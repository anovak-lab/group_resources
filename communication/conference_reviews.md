# Reviewing Conference Papers

Reviewing conference papers is an important service that we all rely on in the nuclear
community. Below is some general information about what constitutes a review of a
conference paper.

## Paper Ranking

You will rank the paper in terms of one of usually four categories:

- Accept: this paper is great as-is, no changes needed
- Accept with revisions: paper is great, I recommend some revisions but they're not so critical to hold up publication of the paper if not implemented
- Reject unless revised: there are some more serious flaws/things needing improved before the paper can be published. If not undertaken, paper will be rejected.
- Reject: very flawed, incomplete, not to publish

## Feedback to the Authors

Then, you'll want to give specific feedback for the paper - usually this is a set of bullet points of comments and questions, for things like

- Areas that needed more clarification/things you did not understand (and which the average reader therefore might not understand)
- Requests for additional analysis
- Discussion on the assumptions made/limitations of the work
- Expanding a literature review to be more holistic
- Better motivating the work
- Typos/grammar
